Fatal Four — How Everything Works

Build Bubble

Select every team you think can reach the CFP semifinals. You can edit your Bubble until TCU–UNC kickoff. The smallest Bubble containing all four semifinalists wins.

AP Poll

The AP Top 25 shown here is the official poll. New official weeks replace the current week while previous released weeks remain available. Future weeks never appear early.

Semifinal chances

These are projections, not guarantees. The production model can incorporate results, schedule strength, injuries, coaching changes and other current information. We will not present invented percentages as official facts.

Power Rankings

The rankings estimate which Bubbles are most likely to contain all four semifinalists. Everyone is included; the page shows 10, then 100, then all.

Explore, follows, likes and messages

After the Bubble lock, users can discover public Bubbles, follow players, like Bubbles, visit profiles and DM users.

Groups

Create a group with a title and join code. Members get a group leaderboard and group chat.

Sign in to Fatal Four

A returning user can sign in in a few seconds. New users can create an account and keep their session on this device.
