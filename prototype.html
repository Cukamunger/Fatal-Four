Fatal Four — Complete Tutorial & FAQ

How do I play?

Choose the smallest group of teams you believe can produce the four CFP semifinalists. Your Bubble can be edited until kickoff.

When do Bubbles lock?

At TCU–UNC kickoff on August 29, 2026 at 12:00 PM ET. Before that moment, other users cannot see your Bubble.

What do the team stats mean?

AP Rank is the official AP position when available. Overall Record is the team's current record. Conference Rank and Conference Record describe conference standing. Semifinal Chance is a model estimate, not a guarantee.

Why can I browse without signing in?

Browsing should be easy. Signing in is only necessary to save your Bubble, follow users, like Bubbles, message people or use account features.

What is Power Bubble Rankings?

It ranks every Bubble by its estimated chance of covering the eventual four semifinalists. The page shows 10, then 100, then all entries.

What is Most Picked?

It shows how often each eligible FBS school appears in submitted Bubbles. 0% means nobody has selected that school in the available submitted data.

How does the AP Poll work?

The site never invents future AP weeks. It shows the current official release and keeps previous released weeks available.

How do Groups work?

Create a named group, share its code, see the member count and your rank, use the leaderboard and group chat, upload a group image, and leave a group. The owner can set name/photo permissions and an optional member limit. Other members' Bubbles remain hidden until the deadline.

What does the blue Bubble mean in Explore?

It is only an Explore-page indicator showing that a team is in your Bubble.
